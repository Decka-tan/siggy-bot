# How I Won the Siggy Bot Competition

---

So Ritual announced this Siggy Bot Competition back in March 2026. For those who don't know, Siggy's the black cat mascot — cute but kinda generic, just another crypto mascot doing mascot things.

The rules were simple: build a bot. But when I looked at the early entries, I noticed something. They were all... fine. Like, technically functional bots with AI chat and analytics and whatever. But none of them were memorable.

Ritual's community has seen everything at this point. Another "useful" bot isn't gonna impress anyone. I needed to do something different.

---

## The Anime Girl Idea

Here's my thought process: some people in Ritual like anime. Not everyone — but enough. If I could make those people excited, that's votes. That's attention.

Second thought: a memorable character beats a useful tool.

So instead of building "Siggy Bot" — I'd build **Siggy**. A character who happens to be accessible through a bot.

Not just responses, but personality. Not just a chat interface, but a visual novel experience. Not just a mascot, but a character with an actual backstory.

---

## Why Megamind Was Right

There's this scene in Megamind where he shouts "PRESENTATION!!" and it stuck with me.

The presentation layer matters as much as the features. Think about it — a bot can have amazing architecture and sub-100ms response times, but if it feels like interacting with a terminal, no one's gonna remember it.

But a bot with mid features and EXCEPTIONAL presentation? People talk about that. They share screenshots.

So I went all in.

---

## 30 Days of Building

**March 9** — Initial commit. Just a basic Discord bot. Nothing special.

Later that day — complete redesign. The initial direction was wrong.

Evening — added the Bio component. First step in making Siggy feel like a character, not just a bot.

**March 10** — The big pivot. I added Story Mode. A full visual novel with 4 chapters:

- Chapter 1: The Cosmic Void — Siggy's origin as a multidimensional cat entity
- Chapter 2: The Descent — Her journey to Earth, losing parts of herself
- Chapter 3: First Contact — First encounters with humans
- Chapter 4: Becoming Real — Realizing interaction makes her more "real"

Each chapter had choices, mood badges, branching paths. This wasn't flavor text — it was establishing Siggy's IDENTITY.

**Week 2** — Built the knowledge base. Scraped 803+ events from Ritual's Discord. Compiled everything from July 2025 to March 2026. Now Siggy wasn't making things up — she actually knew the community.

Also added multi-conversation support (ChatGPT-style sidebar), message actions, markdown support.

**Week 3** — VN Mode. This was the game changer.

Sprites that change expression based on context. Backgrounds that rotate. Anime-style dialogue boxes with typewriter effects. Quick reply bubbles. Sound effects.

The technical challenge was real. Managing sprite state, mobile responsiveness, making sure the typewriter effect didn't feel slow. Changed the color scheme from generic green to "Siggy Golden Eye" yellow. Fixed like a million z-index bugs.

**Week 4** — Discord integration. Built the `/check` command that shows user stats with AI-powered personality analysis. Added an invoice system because Ritual communities do shared expenses. Bug fixes, polish, more bug fixes.

**Total: 660 commits.**

---

## What I Learned

Character before features. I didn't start with "what commands should this have?" — I started with "who IS Siggy?"

The features followed from her identity. Story Mode wasn't added because visual novels are cool — it was added to establish her backstory. VN Mode wasn't just aesthetic — it was the natural evolution of "what would it feel like to actually TALK to Siggy?"

Personality wins. A bot with amazing architecture but generic personality loses to a bot with mid architecture but great personality every time.

The details are the difference. Anyone can build a chat interface. Not everyone thinks about typewriter effects that feel authentic, or sprite expressions that match context, or color schemes that reinforce character identity.

Go all in or go home. Either you design proper sprites, write actual dialogue, build real narrative — or you don't do it at all.

## So Did It Work?

March 26, 2026 — announcement drops.

"Out of THOUSANDS we have selected a top 10... ONLY THREE REMAIN."

Final 3: me, Yourinuu, Gengar.

72 hours of voting. Community tested all three bots.

The strategy worked. The anime girl transformation, the Story Mode, the VN aesthetic, the all-in commitment to presentation — it wasn't just different. It was better.

People didn't just use the bot — they engaged with Siggy as a character. Story Mode got completion runs. VN Mode became the preferred chat interface. Screenshots got shared. People voted.
